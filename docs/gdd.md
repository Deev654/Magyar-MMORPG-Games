# Magyar MMORPG Game Design Document

## 1. Core Pillars
- **Community & Cooperation:** Encourage players to collaborate in guilds, shared housing, and large-scale events reflecting Hungarian folklore.
- **Dynamic Storytelling:** Narrative arcs influenced by player decisions and seasonal world states.
- **Skill-Based Combat:** Hybrid action-targeting combat rewarding positioning and timing.
- **Living Economy:** Crafting, trading, and territorial control shape regional prosperity.

## 2. Moment-to-Moment Gameplay
1. **Exploration:** Open-world traversal across historical and mythical Hungarian regions. Players discover shrines, hidden villages, and procedurally refreshed points of interest.
2. **Combat:** Fast-paced engagements with light/heavy attacks, spell glyphs, dodges, and counter windows. Enemies telegraph attacks with color-coded tells.
3. **Social Interaction:** Emotes, bard performances, tavern games, and player-run market stalls.
4. **Progression Loops:** Daily contracts, storyline quests, and faction objectives feed into reputation and territory control.

## 3. Combat Mechanics
- **Hybrid Targeting:** Soft lock-on with manual aiming for projectiles and cone attacks.
- **Stamina & Focus:** Stamina governs physical actions; Focus fuels spells and counters.
- **Glyph Combos:** Players assemble runic sequences mid-combat to trigger elemental effects.
- **Enemy AI:** Uses behavior trees reacting to player roles and threat levels.

## 4. Progression Systems
### 4.1 Character Growth
- **Classless Skill Web:** Players unlock nodes by spending Knowledge Points earned via leveling and discoveries.
- **Proficiencies:** Weapon and crafting proficiencies level independently based on use.
- **Heritage Traits:** Story choices grant permanent modifiers and unique abilities tied to folklore creatures.

### 4.2 Gear & Power
- **Item Level Bands:** Gear tiers gated by renown milestones. Items scale via upgrade essences gathered in dungeons.
- **Set Resonance:** Matching folklore sets unlock resonance bonuses (aura, stat boosts).

### 4.3 Reputation & Factions
- **Regional Factions:** Villages, guilds, and mythic entities offer questlines and perks. Reputation unlocks vendors, blueprints, and regional buffs.
- **Territory Control:** Guilds capture fortresses; ownership grants taxation rights and defensive buffs. Weekly sieges reset control opportunities.

### 4.4 Seasonal Content
- **Seasonal Arc:** Every 12 weeks new mythic threat emerges with limited-time mechanics.
- **Battle Chronicle:** Seasonal achievements tracked across server; top contributors earn legacy cosmetics.

## 5. Content Structure
- **Main Story Chapters:** Multi-stage campaigns culminating in large raid encounters.
- **Side Activities:** Fishing in the Tisza, horse archery challenges, rune puzzles.
- **World Events:** Dynamic "Táltos Storms" altering zones, spawning rare bosses and resource nodes.

## 6. Player Housing
- **Village Plots:** Instanced neighborhoods with communal upgrades.
- **Customization:** Modular building pieces, cultural décor, functional crafting stations.
- **Progression Tie-In:** Housing upgrades provide buffs, storage, and NPC services.

## 7. PvP Systems
- **Battlegrounds:** Structured 10v10 arena with objective-based matches.
- **Open-World Flagging:** Optional regional conflicts with risk-reward incentives.
- **Duel Circles:** Designated arenas enabling spectators and betting.

## 8. Monetization & Live Ops
- **Cosmetics Only:** Skins, emotes, mounts; no pay-to-win items.
- **Season Pass:** Track for cosmetic rewards and convenience items.
- **Live Events:** Community festivals tied to Hungarian holidays.

## 9. Accessibility & UX
- Customizable UI, colorblind-friendly telegraphs, scalable text, and narrated quest text.

## 10. Future Expansion Hooks
- Underworld expansion, cross-faction alliances, airborne transportation systems.
